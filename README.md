# 🎓 IUB Exam Preparation Portal (250 MCQs)
### The Islamia University of Bahawalpur — Department of Software Engineering

An interactive, responsive, self-contained exam preparation and revision portal with **250 verified MCQs**, instant score checking, automatic answer validation, and a persistent **Mistake Diary**.

---

## 📚 Included Subjects

1. **Software Verification & Validation (Testing & QA)** — `150 MCQs`
   - Introduction to V&V & Core Testing Principles (Q1 – Q25)
   - Software Quality, Roles & STLC (Q26 – Q50)
   - Use Case Modeling – Part I (Q51 – Q75)
   - Use Case Modeling – Part II & ATM Case Study (Q76 – Q100)
   - Mutation Testing & Fault Injection (Q101 – Q125)
   - Testing Levels & Integration Testing (Q126 – Q150)

2. **Web Engineering** — `100 MCQs`
   - Web & Internet Fundamentals (Q1 – Q7)
   - Web Architecture & Components (Q8 – Q14)
   - Static vs. Dynamic Websites (Q15 – Q21)
   - Internet Services (FTP & VoIP) (Q22 – Q25)
   - SRS & Requirements Engineering (Q26 – Q45)
   - Request-Response & Web Protocols (Q46 – Q55)
   - Multi-Tier Architecture (2-Tier & 3-Tier) (Q56 – Q65)
   - Web Stack (Front-End, Back-End, DBMS) (Q66 – Q79)
   - Network Protocols, Ports & HTTP Methods (Q80 – Q100)

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Instant Feedback & Highlighting**:
  - Wrong answers highlight in **RED** with a `✗ Your Choice (Wrong)` badge.
  - Correct answers highlight in **GREEN** with a `✓ Correct Answer` badge.
  - High-yield explanations appear immediately after selecting an answer.
* **Smart Mistake Diary**:
  - Automatically records every question you get wrong.
  - Drill mode: Retest **only your mistakes** until you reach 100% accuracy.
  - Add your own study notes and mnemonics.
  - Persisted in browser `localStorage`.
* **Exam Simulation**:
  - Timed countdown timer (90 mins for SVV, 60 mins for Web Eng, 150 mins for Mega Exam).
  - 1–150 / 1–100 Question Navigator Palette with live status badges.
  - Flagging for review and submit confirmation.
* **Score Checker & Analytics**:
  - Visual circular score meter with percentage and pass/fail indicators.
  - Topic-wise breakdown bars showing strengths and weak areas.
  - Printable score report.
* **Self-Contained & Offline Ready**:
  - Pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript with synthesized audio effects. No external build steps or CDNs required.
